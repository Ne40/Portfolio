WHAT'S UP

A little more about how I work, what I can help with, and the technical background behind my freelance services.

computer science + practical data work

I like making messy data easier to work with.

I help clients with the practical data tasks that often take more time than they should — entering and organizing information, cleaning Excel or CSV files, checking data quality, exploring datasets, creating straightforward visualizations, and automating repetitive steps with Python when it makes sense.

My Computer Science background gives me a technical foundation in programming, databases, and software projects, while my freelance positioning stays focused on useful, realistic support that I can deliver carefully and reliably.

I pay attention to small details, check my work, and try to understand what the client actually needs before choosing a solution. If something can be made simpler, clearer, or easier to repeat, that is usually where I can help.
